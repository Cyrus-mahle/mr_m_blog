Using this site

Anyone may read the blog. You need an account to write posts. You are responsible for what you publish. Do not post unlawful, abusive or misleading content, and do not post anyone's private information. The administrator may remove posts that break these rules.

Your account and security

Choose a strong password and keep it private. We store passwords hashed, so nobody at {{ site.name }} can read them. We limit login attempts to protect your account.

Your posts

You keep ownership of your posts. You can edit or delete them at any time. The administrator can edit or delete any post.

Cookies and tracking
